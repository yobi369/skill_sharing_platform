Privacy notice

Your information, clearly explained.

Last updated: 28 October 2026. This notice describes the current SkillChain application. It is a product notice, not legal advice.

Information we store

  • Account display name, email address, password hash, account status and role.
  • Skill listings, exchange requests, in-app notifications and reports submitted to moderators.
  • Short-lived, hashed email-verification and password-reset tokens with expiry timestamps.
  • Operational request logs and request IDs needed to diagnose service failures.

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; raw passwords are not stored. One-time security tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes.

How information is used

We use account data to operate sign-in, publish listings, connect learning requests, notify participants, prevent abuse, and review reports. Publicly published skill listings may show the display name of the listing owner. Email addresses and password hashes are not part of public listings.

Service providers and delivery

The service requires a PostgreSQL database and may use an SMTP provider to send verification and password-reset messages. Hosting and database providers process data to operate the service. Configure production providers and their privacy terms before opening registration to the public.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account from the signed-in workspace. This deletes the account and related listings, requests, verification/reset tokens, and notifications according to database relationships. Some moderation reports may remain to preserve the context of a safety review, without a direct foreign-key link to a deleted target. Database backups, if enabled by an operator, may retain data until their documented retention period expires.

Security and contact

The project uses password hashing, time-limited one-use security tokens, access checks, and input validation, but no system can promise perfect security. Before launch, the operator should publish a monitored support/privacy contact and define jurisdiction-specific privacy rights, retention periods, and breach-response procedures. No support email address is published here because one has not been configured.
