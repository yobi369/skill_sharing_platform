What SkillChain provides

SkillChain helps members publish skills they can share and send peer-to-peer learning requests. A listing or accepted request is not a guarantee of skill, availability, outcome, identity, or safety.

Member responsibilities

  • Provide accurate account and listing information.
  • Keep login credentials private and use a unique password.
  • Communicate respectfully and do not harass, defraud, impersonate, spam, or publish unlawful or unsafe content.
  • Only publish content you have permission to share and do not disclose another person's private information.
  • Report suspected abuse through the in-product reporting flow.

Moderation and account closure

Authorized moderators may review reports and may archive reported listings or suspend accounts when necessary. You may delete your account from the workspace. Abuse of reporting tools may itself be subject to moderation.

No payments or guarantees

The current product does not process payments, escrow, cryptocurrency, or blockchain transactions. Members arrange learning interactions directly and should not share sensitive financial or identity information through listing messages.

The service is provided as available, may change, and is not a substitute for professional advice or verified credentials.
