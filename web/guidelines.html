Community safety

Make learning safe and useful.

SkillChain is for respectful peer-to-peer learning. These guidelines apply to profiles, skill listings, and learning requests.

Expected behaviour

  • Describe your skills and experience honestly. Do not claim credentials you do not have.
  • Be respectful, inclusive, and clear about availability and expectations.
  • Keep personal contact details private until you are comfortable sharing them.
  • Never ask another member for passwords, one-time codes, payment card details, or unnecessary identity documents.

Not allowed

  • Threats, harassment, hate, stalking, or targeted abuse.
  • Fraud, scams, impersonation, phishing, or requests for illegal activity.
  • Spam, deceptive advertising, fabricated reviews, or misleading listings.
  • Content that exposes private information without permission or creates a credible safety risk.

How to report

Sign in, choose “Report listing” or “Report member,” select the closest reason, and describe the concern with enough detail for a moderator to review it. Submit reports in good faith. Do not use reports to retaliate against another member.

What happens next

Reports enter a moderator queue. Moderators can mark a report as reviewing, resolve it, dismiss it, or archive a reported skill/suspend a reported account. The product does not yet provide an appeal workflow or a guaranteed response time. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services rather than relying on this website.
